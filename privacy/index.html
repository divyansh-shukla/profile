Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

This site is the personal site of Divyansh Shukla. It has an AI assistant that answers questions about his work and can schedule meetings or send emails on his behalf.

Visitors

You do not need an account or a login to use the site. This site does not keep a database of visitors' details and does not save chat transcripts. Messages you type to the assistant are sent to an AI model provider to generate replies. If you ask the assistant to book a meeting or send a message, the details you give (such as your name, email address, and message) are used to create a calendar event or send an email on the owner's behalf. The site's host may keep standard technical logs of requests. Your information is not sold or used for advertising.

Google user data

The assistant accesses the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar, only to read and organize his email, send emails, and manage his calendar. This data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with others.

The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
